Privacy policy

This page is a starting draft based on the APK manifest supplied for this website. It must be reviewed and completed against the app’s actual runtime behavior before being treated as the publisher’s final privacy notice.

Publisher action required: The APK manifest shows declared permissions and integrated Android/Google service components, but it does not reveal all data collected, transmitted, stored or shared by the running app. Confirm those details and add a monitored privacy contact before publishing this policy as final.

1. Who is responsible?

This application is referred to as “Viewny” in this website. Before publication, the publisher must add its full legal or trading name, business address (where required), and a working privacy contact address. Until those details are added, this draft does not identify the data controller or publisher.

2. Information this app may process

The APK’s manifest alone is not enough to determine the personal information the app processes. The publisher must inspect the app’s code, backend services, SDK configuration and network behavior, then list the exact categories of information collected or generated. Do not publish a blanket “we collect no data” statement unless that has been verified.

Document, as applicable: account and sign-in details; device or app identifiers; usage and diagnostic information; crash logs; content users submit; notification tokens; and information received from connected services.

3. Permissions declared by the supplied APK

The supplied manifest declares these permissions:

  • INTERNET — permits network access.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — permits checking network connectivity state.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — relates to posting notifications on supported Android versions.
  • com.google.android.providers.gsf.permission.READ_GSERVICES — a Google Services Framework permission.

A declared permission does not by itself prove that a particular personal-data operation occurs. The publisher should explain which features use each permission and whether the app accesses any additional data at runtime.

4. Google and third-party services

The supplied APK contains Firebase and Google Play Services-related components. The publisher must identify which services are actually initialized and used, what information they receive, the purposes of processing, and where users can read the relevant third-party privacy notices. Do not assume that an included SDK is active, or that an inactive SDK has no effect, without checking the app configuration and runtime behavior.

5. How information is used and shared

Before release, specify the purposes for each data category (for example, account access, core app functionality, notifications, diagnostics or fraud prevention), the lawful basis where applicable, and every recipient or processor that receives information. Identify whether information is transferred internationally and what safeguards are used, where required.

6. Retention, security and deletion

The publisher should state how long each category of information is retained, how users can request access or deletion, and what happens to backups and records that must be kept for legal or security reasons. Describe the technical and organizational safeguards actually implemented; do not make absolute guarantees of security.

7. Children’s privacy

The publisher must determine whether Viewny is directed to children or knowingly collects information from children, and add the age-related disclosures, consent process and safeguards required by the laws that apply to the service.

8. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, update the effective date and explain material changes in a way users can find. Where required, notify users or obtain consent before applying changes to existing processing.

9. Contact

Add a monitored privacy email address and the publisher’s legal identity here before launch. Do not leave this section blank in a public release.

10. Important note

This draft is provided as website content, not legal advice. The publisher is responsible for verifying the app’s real data practices and adapting this notice to the applicable jurisdictions before publication.
